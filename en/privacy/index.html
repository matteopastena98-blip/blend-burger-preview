What this site does

  • It uses no cookies, ours or anyone else’s. That is why there is no banner.
  • It has no forms: we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • It has no visitor analytics. If we ever add them, they will be cookieless and aggregated, and we will say so here first.
  • It loads no maps, videos, fonts or scripts from other sites: everything comes from the site’s own address.

Like any website, the server hosting it may log technical request data (IP address, date and time, page requested) to keep it running and secure.

When you leave the site

The Order button takes you to blend-napoli.it, the service you order through: its own terms and privacy notice apply there. The link only carries parameters saying which button on our site you came from (for example utm_content=menu), nothing about you.

Instagram, Google Maps and WhatsApp open on their own services, under their own rules.

When you message us

If you call us or message us on WhatsApp, including to send a CV, we use your details only to reply and, for CVs, to consider your application. We do not sell them or use them for advertising.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data, or to stop using it (GDPR, Articles 15–22), using the contacts above. You can also complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante (garanteprivacy.it).
